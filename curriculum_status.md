## Key Principles Established
1. **Every class = she makes something** she can show someone
2. **45–60 min sessions**, at least half is her doing, not you talking
3. **Follow her interests** (K-dramas, aesthetics) as hooks into tools
4. **One or two new tools per week max** — depth over breadth
5. **Teach-back**: End every session with her explaining in her own words
6. **Prompting is THE skill** — side-by-side bad vs good prompt demos
